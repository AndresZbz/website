# Graduated from College. Now what?

After about five years of studying, I’ve finally finished my Computer Science degree (just waiting on the graduation ceremony to pick up my diploma!). Now, the big question on my mind is: "Now what?"

Studying was one of the main ways I kept my brain engaged. Without it, I’m looking for new ways to stay active, whether that’s diving into personal projects, learning new skills, or picking up new hobbies. Fortunately, I already have a job so job hunting isn't a priority.

I really want to make sure I’m using my time meaningfully, rather than falling into a simple loop of working and resting. I have plenty of personal projects I want to finish, plus I want to make space for self-care, working out, and spending time with people I care about. I’m also grateful to count some of those people as my coworkers; even though working together can be challenging at times, having them around definitely makes the day-to-day work feel lighter.

My to-do list right now is filled with tasks of my own projects, I will continue them, sharing them and continue hanging out with friends, maybe finishing college is not important for some people, but for others is a new way to look onto their life, for myself, I plan on continue doing what I do till I hit a wall or feel burned down about everything related to software engineering.

Thats all. cya!
